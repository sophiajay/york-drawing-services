Who Needs a Floor Plan?

A good floor plan is one of the most useful documents you can have for a property. Our clients use them for:

  • Planning a renovation or extension - an accurate starting point for builders, kitchen fitters and designers
  • Selling or letting a property – buyers and tenants want to see how a home flows before they book a viewing
  • HMO licence applications – clear room layouts with dimensions for your licensing paperwork
  • Interior design and space planning - – test furniture layouts and room changes before you spend money
  • Building control and insurance – a reliable record of your property as it stands today
  • Commercial premises – offices, shops and studios that need layout or fit-out planning

Types of Floor Plans We Produce

Existing (as-built) floor plans

A measured record of your property exactly as it is now, including walls, doors, windows, stairs and fixed fittings.

Proposed floor plans

New layouts showing how your home could look after an extension, loft conversion or internal remodel. See our House Extensions, Loft Conversions and Garage Conversions pages for full planning packages.

Estate agent and marketing floor plans

Clean, easy-to-read plans with room names, dimensions and total floor area, ready to add to a property listing.

HMO and licensing floor plans

Scaled plans showing room uses, sizes and layout, prepared for licence applications to City of York Council.

Space planning and furniture layouts

Plans showing how furniture, kitchens and bathrooms will fit, so you can make confident decisions before work starts.
